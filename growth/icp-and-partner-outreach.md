# ICP + partner outreach (member-value angle, not backlink angle)

Distinct from `growth/outreach-crm.md` (press/backlink outreach). This is
**customer acquisition** outreach — pitching gatekeepers to share LandlordKit
with their members as a free benefit, which puts real prospective Pro
customers in front of the product.

## ICP

**Primary: self-managing small landlord, 2–10 units, no property manager.**
Recurring compliance pain (multiple tenants = multiple deposit deadlines,
rent reviews, late fees) — matches Pro's batch generation, saved profile, and
deadline-reminder features directly. Found in: r/Landlord, BiggerPockets,
local REIA (real estate investor association) meetups, state/city landlord
associations.

**Secondary: first-time/accidental landlord** (inherited property, converted
primary residence, first rental). High search intent right now, lower
recurring value — this is the SEO/content audience, not the primary Pro-
conversion target. Don't build partner outreach around this segment; the free
tools + SEO already reach them.

## Why partner outreach (not cold email to individuals)

No purchasable/scrapeable list of "landlords with 2–10 units" exists, and
cold-emailing individual landlords reads as spam with no reply path. Landlord
associations and REIA chapters are **organizations with real contact points**
that already reach hundreds/thousands of exactly this ICP — one email to an
association can put the tool in front of its whole membership.

## Targets (reuses/tightens Tier 1 of outreach-crm.md, but for a different ask)

Same organizations as `growth/outreach-crm.md` Tier 1 — but the pitch here is
"share this with your members," not "link to us for SEO." Verify each is
still active before sending (site/newsletter check, 2 min).

- State/city landlord associations for the best-covered states: MA
  (MassLandlords), NY, FL, TX, CO, ME, IL, WA (all have verified /laws hubs)
- Local REIA chapters (search "<city> real estate investors association") —
  these run in-person meetups + email lists, ideal for a member-benefit pitch
- BiggerPockets forums/groups — not a cold pitch, but genuine participation
  (see `growth/forum-answer-bank.md` rules) that surfaces the tool naturally
- Local landlord Facebook groups / Nextdoor landlord groups — smaller, but
  free and fast to test

## Outreach templates

### 1. Association/REIA newsletter — member benefit pitch
**Subject:** Free tool for your members — state-cited landlord compliance calculators

Hi [Name] — I run LandlordKit, free calculators for small landlords (security
deposit interest, late-fee caps, rent-increase notices, a compliance-deadline
calendar) — every rule cites the actual statute with a last-verified date, no
signup required.

Landlords always ask basic compliance questions in [Association]'s
group/newsletter — this might save your team time answering them, and it's a
genuinely useful free resource for members with 2–10 units doing their own
paperwork. Happy to send a one-paragraph blurb for your next newsletter, or a
[State]-specific link if that's more useful ([state law hub link]).

No cost, no catch — I built it because I'm a small landlord too and the
"free" alternatives were ad farms or wrong about state law.

— Jake, LandlordKit

### 2. REIA meetup organizer — offer to speak / demo
**Subject:** 5-minute compliance-tools demo for your next meetup?

Hi [Name] — saw [chapter]'s meetup schedule. I built a free set of
statute-cited compliance tools for small landlords (deposit deadlines,
late-fee caps, a filing calendar with automatic reminders) and would be happy
to do a quick 5-minute demo or just drop a link in your resources — whichever
is more useful to your group. No pitch, no cost; genuinely built this because
these are the questions that come up at every meetup I've been to.

— Jake, LandlordKit

### 3. Local Facebook/Nextdoor landlord group — direct member post
(Post as yourself, disclosing you built it — same rule as Reddit.)

> Built a free tool for fellow landlords here: state-specific calculators for
> security deposit interest, late fees, and rent-increase notice periods —
> every rule cites the actual statute + when I last checked it (a lot of the
> "free" sites online are just wrong, especially since several states changed
> their rules in the last year). Also has a compliance calendar that reminds
> you before 1099/tax/filing deadlines. [link] — feedback welcome, I'm one of
> you.

## Cadence
2–3 association/REIA emails per week (same email-finding rule as
outreach-crm.md: find the current contact from the org's own site each time).
1 local Facebook/Nextdoor group post per week, rotating groups — never repeat
the same text across groups.
